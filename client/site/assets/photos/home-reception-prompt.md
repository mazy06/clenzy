## Prompt final

Create a single premium editorial hospitality photograph for Baitly's website hero, aspect ratio 1:1, photorealistic, high quality. A believable candid moment at a boutique guesthouse reception, seen from behind and three quarters to the side of a professional woman host in her thirties with dark hair loosely tied back and a soft muted blue linen shirt. She is seated on the LEFT side of the composition at a refined warm walnut reception counter, her face in natural partial profile, calmly working with her right hand on a mouse. A large elegant slim desktop monitor is CENTER RIGHT, clearly facing the camera, with its full display unobscured. The screen must show the Baitly property reservation planning interface from the attached reference: faithful dark navy narrow left sidebar with the Baitly line house logo, white Planning header, pale blue-grey calendar grid, small property thumbnails in the left column, dated columns, rounded tan, caramel brown and dark brown horizontal reservation bars with small status badges. Reproduce the reference UI convincingly inside the actual physical screen, not floating over the photo, no giant text or invented graphs. Screen should occupy about 42% of image width and be sharp and readable as a planning interface. Person and monitor both fully inside middle 80% of frame to survive responsive crop. Environment: inviting upscale small reception with warm limestone plaster walls, a subtle archway and out-of-focus courtyard greenery in the background, discreet desk bell and a closed notebook. Contemporary Moroccan/Mediterranean hospitality, not an office cubicle. Natural morning daylight from right, soft shadows, realistic skin and hands, honest linen and wood textures, restrained sand/walnut/blue-grey colors. Editorial photography shot on 50mm lens at f/4, slight depth of field, screen and person's profile sharp. Leave uppermost 15% calm wall background and bottom 15% simple counter for later website overlays. Natural scene, not a staged stock-photo handshake, no person looking at camera, no floating UI, no overlay captions, no watermarks, no large signage, no Apple logo. The reference is ONLY the software interface to show on the monitor; generate the rest of the photographic scene from this brief.
